01｜One-Five, Not Five-Zero｜先确认数字

Mei reaches the check-in desk with her passport already open. The agent lifts a tag from the printer.

“Your bag is checked through to London on this booking.”

“So I don’t need to collect it during the transfer?”

“That’s right. Boarding starts at six fifteen.”

“Six fifty?”

“Six fifteen. One-five.”

Mei checks the boarding pass and repeats the time. After security, she asks for directions to the gate instead of following the busiest crowd.

The following afternoon, rain follows her into the hotel.

“I have a reservation under the name Mei Chen. Is breakfast included?”

The receptionist checks. “Yes. Check-out is at eleven.”

Upstairs, there is no hot water. Mei calls the desk, gives her room number, and asks when someone can come. She is tired, but this time the important number is the one she says first: room 812.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
checked through to … 行李直挂到……，针对具体预订确认。
boarding starts at … ……开始登机，不是起飞。
under the name … 以……的名字预订。
There’s no hot water in my room. 我的房间没有热水。

听辨：fifteen 是十五，fifty 是五十；重要时间核对票面。

展开：口语／写作练习

询问行李是否直挂，再向酒店说明房间问题。

参考表达：Will I need to collect my bag during the transfer? / I’m in room 812. There’s no hot water. Could someone take a look?

02｜The Door Marked Staff Only｜病区第一天

Emma meets Mei by the ward entrance. Mei has brought a notebook, two pens, and far too many questions.

“Let me show you around,” Emma says. “The staff room is at the end of the corridor.”

At the nurses’ station, phones ring while someone checks the shift roster. Mei hears three unfamiliar abbreviations before she has written the first one down.

“Could you explain what that stands for?”

Emma slows down. “Of course. And today you’re observing. Check with me before joining any patient care.”

“Who should I speak to if I’m unsure about something?”

“Start with me. Priya is the nurse in charge.”

Before entering a patient’s room, Emma introduces Mei and asks whether he is comfortable with an observer being present.

Mei closes her notebook. For the next few minutes, listening is the whole job.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
show someone around 带某人熟悉环境。
What does that stand for? 那个缩写代表什么？
the nurse in charge 当班负责护士，具体岗位名称随机构而异。
an observer 观察交流人员。

语境：观察交流与获准独立执业是不同身份；这里先说明 Mei 的角色。

展开：口语／写作练习

用两句介绍自己，再询问不确定的事情该找谁。

参考表达：I’m Mei, a visiting nurse observing the team today. Who should I speak to if I have questions?

03｜Before the Cuff Goes On｜测量之前

Several months later, Mei returns after completing the required registration and induction. Emma is beside her for the first supported shifts.

Mr Brown looks up as they approach.

“Hello, I’m Mei, one of the nurses looking after you today. How would you like me to address you?”

“James is fine.”

Mei asks him to state his full name and date of birth, then checks them against the identification details used by the ward.

“I’d like to check your blood pressure. The cuff will tighten around your arm for a short time. Is that okay?”

He nods, then looks toward the open curtain.

“Could you close that first?”

“Of course.”

When she comes back, he asks how long it will take. Mei explains before reaching for the cuff. His shoulders relax. It was a small question, but he had needed an answer.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
look after someone 照护某人。
date of birth 出生日期。
blood pressure cuff 血压袖带。
The cuff will tighten. 袖带会收紧。

区别：核对身份的信息与房号不同；解释、征询意见和保护隐私是沟通的一部分。参考：NMC 职业规范。

展开：口语／写作练习

向患者介绍自己，解释测血压时会有什么感觉。

参考表达：I’m one of the nurses looking after you today. I’d like to check your blood pressure. The cuff will tighten briefly around your arm.

04｜The Question After “Six”｜疼痛评分之后

The next morning, James leaves his breakfast untouched.

“Are you feeling sick?” Mei asks.

“My stomach hurts.”

She pulls up a chair. “Could you show me exactly where?”

He points to the right side of his abdomen.

“When did it start?”

“About an hour ago.”

“Is it there all the time, or does it come and go?”

“It comes and goes.”

Mei explains the pain scale before asking him to rate it. He says six.

“What makes it worse?”

“Moving. And I’m worried something’s wrong.”

Mei had been ready to ask the next question. Instead, she pauses.

“Thank you for telling me. I’ll let the team know about the pain and your concern.”

When she speaks to Emma, she reports the location, timing, pattern, and James’s own description. The number is part of the message, not all of it.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
Does it come and go? 疼痛是时有时无的吗？
constant / intermittent 持续的／间歇性的。
six out of ten 十分中的六分。
What makes it worse? 什么会让它加重？

患者可能说 stomach hurts（肚子疼）；记录时应依据其描述和评估明确位置，不能自动认定为胃部疾病。

展开：口语／写作练习

询问疼痛的位置、起始时间和变化，再用一句话汇报。

参考表达：He reports pain on the right side of his abdomen that started about an hour ago. He says it comes and goes and gets worse with movement.

05｜“It Made Me Feel Strange”｜把过敏史问清楚

At the medication round, Emma and Mei review the chart together. James watches them approach.

“Is that the same medicine I had yesterday?” he asks.

Mei checks the documented prescription before answering. “Let me confirm the details with you.”

When she asks about allergies, James hesitates.

“Something once made me feel strange. I can’t remember the name.”

“What happened when you took it?”

“I had a rash. My daughter might know what it was.”

Mei does not turn that into “no allergies.” She tells Emma what James has said and asks for the information to be clarified before they continue with the medicine in question.

“Thanks for mentioning it,” she tells him. “We’ll check that first.”

He looks relieved. He had thought an uncertain memory might be too unimportant to mention.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
medication round / medication chart 给药巡视或集中给药时段／用药记录表。
a documented prescription 已经记录的处方或医嘱。
What happened when you took it? 你当时用了之后发生了什么？
Let me confirm the details. 我来确认具体信息。

allergy（过敏）、side effect（副作用）和不明确的不适不能仅凭一个词互相替代；故事练习询问和报告。

展开：口语／写作练习

患者记不清药名，请追问反应并向同事说明仍待核实。

参考表达：He reports a previous rash after a medicine, but he can’t remember its name. The details haven’t been confirmed yet.

06｜We Can Pause｜采血和换药

In the treatment room, Mrs Patel keeps her sleeve pulled down.

“I don’t like needles.”

Mei sets the equipment aside. “What worries you most?”

“I fainted once.”

“Thank you for telling me. Let’s discuss that with Emma before we start.”

After the team agrees how to proceed, Mei explains the blood sample and asks whether Mrs Patel is ready. She describes what she may feel without promising it will be painless.

Later, during a dressing change with Emma, another patient pulls his leg back.

“That stings.”

“We can pause,” Mei says, stopping what she is doing. “Could you tell me where it hurts?”

Afterward, Emma asks what she will record.

“What I observed, what the patient reported, and what we did.”

“Exactly.”

Mei writes “patient reports stinging” rather than “patient difficult.” The first tells the next nurse something useful.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
take a blood sample 采集血样。
faint / feel faint 晕厥／感到快要晕倒。
change a dressing 更换敷料；不等于所有情境下的“上药”。
sting / stinging 刺痛／刺痛感。

避免用 It won’t hurt（不会疼）作保证；记录可观察事实及患者原话，避免主观标签。

展开：口语／写作练习

患者说害怕针头，请回应并询问原因；写一句客观记录。

参考表达：What worries you most about the blood test? / Patient states, “I fainted during a previous blood test.” Concern shared with supervising nurse.

07｜The Chair Can Wait｜先把协助说明白

James points toward the chair by the window.

“I’d like to sit over there.”

Mei checks his mobility plan. Earlier, he reported dizziness when standing. She asks Emma to join her rather than trying to manage the move alone.

“Could you help me with James’s transfer? I’d like to confirm the assistance he needs.”

They explain the agreed plan to him before starting.

“There’s no rush,” Mei says. “Please tell us if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable.”

Halfway through the preparation, James says he wants a moment. They pause.

Once he is settled, Mei puts the call bell where he can reach it and checks that his glasses are nearby.

“Anything else you need?”

“My book.”

She hands it to him. The move mattered, but so did the reason he wanted the chair.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
mobility plan / assistance 活动或移动照护计划／协助。
transfer from bed to chair 从床转移到椅子上。
There’s no rush. 不用着急。
within reach 在伸手可及的位置。

transfer 既可指床椅之间的转移，也可指转科或转院，要交代起点和终点。

展开：口语／写作练习

向同事请求移动协助，再向患者说明可以暂停。

参考表达：Could you help me with this transfer? I’d like to check the mobility plan with you. / Please tell us if you need to pause.

08｜Say the Concern First｜先说最担心的事

Mei hears James call her name. His voice sounds different. He is more breathless than he was earlier.

She calls for immediate help using the ward’s escalation process. When Emma arrives, Mei starts with the change rather than the whole morning’s history.

“I’m concerned about James. His breathing has become more difficult.”

She gives his verified identification details, location, relevant background, and the observations available so far, including when they were taken.

“What’s his usual level?” Emma asks.

“I’m checking the documented baseline. I haven’t confirmed it yet.”

The team takes over the urgent assessment. Mei repeats back the task she is assigned, and Emma confirms it.

Later, Mei writes down the timing of the change, whom she contacted, and what followed. The clearest sentence she spoke was also the shortest: “I need help here now.”

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
more breathless than earlier 比之前更气促。
escalate a concern 把担忧及时上报，按流程寻求更高级别支持。
baseline / observations 基线或平时水平／观察与测量结果。
I need an urgent review. 我需要有人尽快进行评估。

SBAR 可组织情况、背景、评估和请求；紧急求助不应等待写完模板。参考：AHRQ SBAR。

展开：口语／写作练习

模拟一次求助，先说变化，再提出明确请求；不编造未测得的数值。

参考表达：I’m concerned about a change in this patient’s breathing. I need you to review him urgently. I’ll give you the observations we have so far.

09｜The Note That Someone Else Reads｜下一班要看懂

By the end of the shift, James has been reviewed and has an updated care plan. Mei opens the handover notes.

“James reported abdominal pain this morning,” she begins. “He was reviewed by the team, and the current plan is documented here.”

Emma points to an earlier draft: “Pain better.”

“Better when?” she asks.

Mei checks her notes. “At the reassessment. He reported that it had decreased from six to three out of ten.”

“And what still needs following up?”

They list the outstanding result, the person responsible for checking it, and the ongoing assistance needs.

At handover, the next nurse asks a question Mei cannot answer from memory.

“I’ll check the record rather than guess.”

She turns the screen so they can read the entry together. A clear note, she realizes, should still make sense when she is no longer in the room.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
reassessment 重新评估。
outstanding result 尚未获得或仍待处理的结果，不是“杰出的结果”。
ongoing assistance needs 持续需要的协助。
document the time and response 记录时间和反应。

记录要区分患者报告、实际观察和已完成行动；时间和待办事项使交班可追踪。

展开：口语／写作练习

把“患者好多了”改写成带时间和信息来源的记录。

参考表达：At 14:30, the patient reported pain of three out of ten, compared with six out of ten at 13:00. Follow-up actions are listed in the care plan.

10｜Not Just Louder｜听不清，也可能听不懂

A colleague gives Mei an update while a trolley rattles past.

“Sorry, I missed the last part. Could we move somewhere quieter?”

Away from the corridor, the colleague repeats the message. Mei reads back the time and task, then waits for confirmation.

Later, Mrs Ahmed answers every question with a polite nod. Mei is not sure they are understanding each other.

“Which language would you prefer to use?”

They arrange an interpreter through the hospital. While waiting, Mei checks whether Mrs Ahmed needs any help hearing or seeing the information.

When the interpreter joins, Mei speaks to Mrs Ahmed directly, using short sentences and allowing time for each response.

For the first time that afternoon, Mrs Ahmed asks a question of her own.

Mei had been trying to make her English simpler. The missing step was asking what communication support the patient needed.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
read back / confirm 复述／确认。
an interpreter / a translator 口译员／笔译者。
Which language would you prefer? 你更希望用哪种语言？
Could we move somewhere quieter? 我们能去安静一点的地方吗？

点头不一定代表理解；语言、听力、视力和环境都可能影响沟通。

展开：口语／写作练习

请求口译协助；把一个含糊的口头任务复述给同事核对。

参考表达：Could you arrange an interpreter for this conversation? / Let me read that back: you’d like me to check the record now and report back to you. Is that correct?

11｜A Daughter at the Door｜家属的担心

James’s daughter arrives carrying a phone charger and a worried expression.

“Is he getting better?” she asks before taking off her coat.

Mei checks with James about including his daughter in the conversation. He says he would like her to stay.

“He’s been reviewed, and we’re following the updated plan,” Mei explains. “What are you most concerned about?”

“Whether this could happen again.”

Mei does not promise that it will not.

“That’s an important question. I’ll ask the clinician to discuss the treatment plan with you both.”

“Will someone come this afternoon?”

“I’ll check who is available and let you know. I don’t have a confirmed time yet.”

She writes down the request and follows it up. When she returns with an update, the daughter thanks her for remembering. A promise about the next conversation was one Mei could actually keep.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
include someone in the conversation 让某人参与谈话。
What are you most concerned about? 你最担心什么？
a confirmed time 已经确认的时间。
discuss the treatment plan 讨论治疗计划。

表达应基于已知信息，并尊重患者的信息分享意愿；不能把“目前平稳”解释成“肯定没事”。

展开：口语／写作练习

家属询问你尚不能确定的治疗结果，请回应并说明下一步。

参考表达：I don’t have a confirmed answer to that. I’ll ask the clinician to discuss it with you and check when they’re available.

12｜Tell Me in Your Own Words｜出院前的复述

On discharge day, James has already put on his coat when Mei brings the written plan.

“I’ll go through this with you before you leave.”

He says he understands, but his eyes keep moving toward the door. Mei takes one section at a time: the confirmed medication instructions, follow-up arrangements, and the symptoms and contact details listed in his plan.

“To check that I’ve explained it clearly, could you tell me how you’ll follow this schedule at home?”

James explains it back. One part does not match the written instructions. Mei goes over it again and asks him to explain the revised understanding.

His daughter writes the appointment date into her phone.

“Who should we contact if we have questions?”

Mei points to the relevant details in the plan. This time, James takes the paper out of the bag and folds it where he can find it.

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
discharge plan / follow-up appointment 出院计划／复诊预约。
in your own words 用你自己的话。
To check that I’ve explained it clearly, … 为了确认我解释清楚了，……。
contact details 联系方式。

teach-back 是请患者用自己的话复述，以检查解释是否被理解，不是考试。参考：AHRQ 照护交接沟通。

展开：口语／写作练习

请患者复述书面安排，避免只问“懂了吗”。

参考表达：To check that I’ve explained it clearly, could you tell me in your own words what the next steps are?

13｜Coffee Before the Next Shift｜午餐与换班

Emma waves Mei over to a table by the window.

“How are you finding things so far?”

Mei thinks about the shift before answering. “I’m getting used to the routine. The phone is still harder than talking face to face.”

“That takes practice. What are you doing on your next day off?”

“Hopefully something that doesn’t involve an alarm clock.”

They laugh. Later, Mei checks the roster and remembers an appointment next Thursday.

“Would you be able to swap shifts with me next Thursday?” she asks Emma.

“Which shift?”

“The late shift. I could take your early one on Friday.”

Emma checks her plans. They agree to ask Priya about approval rather than treating their conversation as the final roster change.

Mei sends a message with both dates and shifts. “Please let us know whether this swap can be approved.”

展开：表达与辨析
英文表达 中文与用法
How are you finding …? 你觉得……怎么样？
get used to the routine 逐渐习惯日常安排。
swap shifts / shift roster 换班／排班表。
the early shift / the late shift 早班／晚班，具体时段以排班表为准。

确认换班时写完整日期和班次，不只说 next Thursday；同事同意与正式排班批准不同。

展开：口语／写作练习

写一条换班申请，包含双方班次和审批请求。

参考表达：Emma and I would like to swap my late shift on [date] with her early shift on [date]. Could you confirm whether this change can be approved?

14｜The Button She Hasn’t Used｜培训时问清楚

At a training session, Mei recognizes the equipment but not the model. The trainer moves through the demonstration quickly.

“Could you show that step again?” Mei asks.

“Of course. Which part?”

展开：听辨练习——电话里的时间

请练习伙伴或朗读工具读下面三句，记录时间、否定和待办项。

  1. The patient was reviewed at fourteen thirty. The result has not come back yet.
  2. The appointment is on Thursday at ten fifteen, not ten fifty.
  3. Her pain was six out of ten at one o’clock. At two o’clock, she reported three out of ten.

核对：① 14:30 已评估，结果尚未返回。② 周四 10:15，不是 10:50。③ 13:00 疼痛六分，14:00 患者报告三分。
