三、连续情景故事：Leo 的两次出发

Leo Chen 是一名软件工程师。Daniel 是伦敦团队的工程师，Sarah 是产品经理，Maya 负责团队的行政协调。第一次短期出差后，Leo 回国远程协作；几个月后，他到伦敦外派一年，认识了邻居 Alex。

01｜The Wrong Queue｜排错的队伍

The queue moves forward. Leo tightens his grip on his passport. On his phone, the message from Daniel is still open: “See you in London tomorrow!”

“Checking in?” a member of staff asks.

“Yes. London, via Doha.”

“You need the next desk. This one's for bag drop.”

At the correct desk, Leo lifts his suitcase onto the scale.

“Will I need to collect this in Doha?”

The agent checks his ticket. “For this booking, it's checked through to London.”

“So I collect it in London?”

“That's right. Your first flight boards at six fifteen, Gate B7.”

“Six fifty?”

“Six fifteen. One-five.” She circles the time on his boarding pass.

Leo thanks her and follows the signs. After the coffee shop, the corridor splits. He stops a passing employee.

“Excuse me, how do I get to B7?”

“Upstairs, then left.”

This time, he repeats the directions before moving. His suitcase is gone, but his shoulders feel lighter.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
bag drop 行李托运柜台；故事中 Leo 还需要先办理值机。
checked through to London 行李直挂伦敦；具体到自己的行程仍需询问。
boarding pass / boarding time 登机牌／开始登机的时间；不要与起飞时间混淆。
So I collect it in London? 所以我在伦敦取行李，对吗？通过复述确认。

听辨：留意 fifteen / fifty。没听清时让对方说成 one-five；重要时间再核对屏幕或票面。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：询问行李是否直挂，以及登机口在哪里。写：给接机同事发两句消息，说明到达时间和地点。

参考表达：Will I need to collect my bag during the transfer? / I’m due to arrive at Terminal 2 at 9 a.m. local time. I’ll message you after I collect my bag.

02｜A Room That Hums｜嗡嗡响的房间

Rain follows Leo into the hotel lobby. He puts his wet backpack beside the desk.

“Hi, I have a reservation under the name Leo Chen.”

The receptionist finds it. “Three nights. Breakfast is from seven to ten.”

“Is that included in my booking?”

“Yes. Check-out is at eleven.”

Upstairs, Leo opens his laptop. The room is warm, and the air conditioner hums without producing any cool air. He waits, then calls reception.

“Hi, I'm in room 812. The air conditioner doesn't seem to be working. Could someone take a look?”

“We'll send someone up.”

“Do you know roughly when?”

“Within twenty minutes.”

Half an hour later, his shirt is still sticking to his back.

“Hi, I'm following up about room 812. Nobody's arrived yet. If it can't be fixed tonight, could I move to another room?”

The receptionist checks. “Yes. We have a room on the floor below.”

Leo closes his laptop. Tomorrow's meeting can wait until he's somewhere cooler.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
under the name … 以……的名字预订。
Is that included in my booking? 我的预订包含这一项吗？
roughly / within twenty minutes 大约／二十分钟内。
I’m following up about … 我想跟进一下……；follow up 是动词短语。

使用提醒：描述事实加上具体请求，比反复说 sorry 或 no problem 更有用。故事里的处理时限只是这家酒店给出的答复。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：你的房间没有热水，已经等了半小时，请提出解决要求。

参考表达：There’s no hot water in room 812. I called thirty minutes ago, but nobody has arrived. Could you check when someone can come?

03｜One Sentence at a Time｜第一场站会

Daniel waves from the kitchen. “Coffee before the stand-up?”

Leo nods. Ten minutes later, everyone is facing a screen full of names.

“Leo, want to go first?”

He sets his cup down carefully. “Yesterday, I checked the login issue. It only happens in production. Today, I'll compare the logs and try to reproduce it locally.”

“Any blockers?”

“I don't have access to the test database yet.”

“Maya can point you to the right person.”

After the meeting, Leo drafts a message, deletes “Please give me permission,” and tries again.

“Hi Maya, I'm investigating the login issue and need read-only access to the test database. Who should I contact?”

She sends him the access request form. By lunchtime, the request is approved.

“Could you try again?” Daniel asks.

Leo runs the query. Rows appear on his screen.

“It works now. Thanks.”

His update was short. Nobody seemed to need a longer one.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
give a quick update 简要同步进展。
reproduce it locally 在本地复现。
a blocker / be blocked by … 阻塞项／被……卡住。
read-only access to … 对……的只读访问权限。

结构：已做什么 → 接下来做什么 → 需要什么帮助。access 通常不可数；说 access to the database。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：用三句做自己的站会更新。写：申请一种明确的权限，并说明用途。

参考表达：Yesterday, I investigated the crash. Today, I’ll check the logs. I’m blocked by a missing test account; could someone help me get one?

04｜Not an Interview｜午餐桌上的聊天

By one o'clock, Leo has forgotten about lunch. Daniel appears beside his desk.

“Want to grab something to eat?”

At the café, the server points to two sandwiches. Leo chooses the chicken one.

“Eat in or takeaway?”

“Sorry, could you say that again?”

“Are you eating here?”

“Oh, here, please.”

They find a small table by the window. Daniel unwraps his sandwich.

“How are you finding London so far?”

Leo almost answers with the name of his map app. Then Daniel adds, “Enjoying it?”

“Yes! Though I haven't seen much beyond the hotel and this office.”

“What do you usually do on weekends?”

“I like walking and taking photos. How about you?”

“I cycle, when it isn't pouring.”

Rain taps the window. They both look outside and laugh.

“Any suggestions for a short walk after work?” Leo asks.

Daniel turns over his receipt and draws a route to the river.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
grab something to eat 去吃点东西；随意的口语。
eat in / takeaway 堂食／外带；这里使用英式表达。
How are you finding …? 你觉得……怎么样？不是问“怎么找到”。
How about you? 你呢？回答后自然把话题交还给对方。

听辨：真实对话常省略主语，如 Want to grab lunch?。练习识别，正式写作中仍可使用完整句。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：用“回答＋一个细节＋反问”聊周末。

参考表达：I usually go for a walk and take a few photos. I’m still exploring the neighborhood. What do you like doing on weekends?

05｜What Does “Everything” Mean?｜把需求问具体

Sarah shares a mock-up. There is one new button: Export.

“Users should be able to download everything,” she says.

Leo looks at the sample table. “By everything, do you mean all records or just the results after filtering?”

“The filtered results.”

“And which format do they need?”

“Excel. Could we finish by Friday?”

Leo pauses. The button looks small. The work behind it does not.

“The API change should take about a day, assuming the data endpoint is ready. We'll also need time for review and validation. Friday is possible, but Monday gives us more room.”

“What can we leave out?” Sarah asks.

“Multiple sheets. We could start with one sheet and keep the selected filters.”

They agree on Monday. Before the call ends, Leo reads back the decision.

“One Excel file, filtered records only, one sheet. I'll confirm the endpoint schedule with the data team today.”

Sarah nods. “Please add that to the ticket.”

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
filtered results 筛选后的结果。
assuming … 假设……；说清估时的前提。
leave something out 暂不包含某项内容。
read back the decision 复述决定，供对方核对。

理解重点：估时不是无条件承诺。把范围、依赖和目标日期一起说清。这里的 validation 泛指验证结果是否符合要求。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

写：整理一条需求确认消息，包含范围、暂不包含的内容、目标日期。

参考表达：To confirm: we’ll export the filtered records to one Excel sheet. Multiple sheets are out of scope for this version. We’re targeting Monday, assuming the endpoint is ready.

06｜A Comment in the Margin｜Review 里的不同意见

A comment appears on Leo's pull request: “Could we simplify this?”

He reads it twice. Then he opens the function and sees how far he has to scroll.

“Do you mean separating validation from file generation?” he replies.

“Yes. That would make the error handling easier to follow.”

Later, Daniel suggests putting the export feature in a new service.

Leo hesitates. “I see the benefit, but I'm concerned about the extra operational work. Traffic is low at the moment. Could we keep it in the existing service for now?”

Daniel leans back. “What if usage grows?”

“We can track the request volume and revisit the decision.”

“Fair point. Add that to the design notes.”

Leo updates the code and leaves a short reply: “I've split the function and added the trade-off to the notes. Could you take another look?”

This time, the response is only three words: “Looks good now.”

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
Could we simplify this? 能否简化这里？常见的协商语气。
I’m concerned about … 我担心……；后面接具体影响。
revisit the decision 在获得新信息后重新评估决定。
a trade-off 取舍；改善一个方面可能付出另一种代价。

语气：I see the benefit, but … 先承认对方方案的价值，再说明担忧和替代方案。LGTM 是 Looks good to me 的常见缩写。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

写：提出一个有原因的 Review 建议，再回应一次建议。

参考表达：Could we move validation into a separate function? That would make the failure cases easier to follow. / Good point. I’ve made that change and updated the PR.

07｜The Red Line｜发布后的红线

At four, Daniel starts the deployment. Leo watches the dashboard while Sarah prepares the announcement.

For a minute, nothing happens. Then the error-rate line rises.

“Hold the announcement,” Daniel says.

Leo opens the logs. “Some users are being logged out after refreshing their session. It started just after the deployment.”

“Do we know why?”

“Not yet. The new validation logic is one possibility, but I haven't confirmed it.”

The line keeps climbing.

“I recommend rolling back,” Leo says. “We can investigate once the service is stable.”

Daniel starts the rollback. Leo writes an update in the incident channel: “Some users are having trouble staying signed in. We're rolling back the latest release. Next update in fifteen minutes.”

Soon, the line begins to fall.

“Back to normal?” Sarah asks.

“The error rate is back to its usual level. We're checking affected sessions before we call it resolved.”

Leo realizes that he has stopped thinking about his accent.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
hold the announcement 先不要发公告。
the error rate / affected users 错误率／受影响的用户。
roll back / a rollback 回滚，动词短语／一次回滚，名词。
call it resolved 认定问题已解决。

准确性：时间上紧接发布不等于已经证实根因。报告应区分影响、事实、推测、措施和下一次更新时间。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

写：写三句故障更新，暂时还不知道根因。

参考表达：Some users can’t stay signed in. We’re investigating and rolling back the latest release. The cause is not yet confirmed; our next update will be at 4:30 p.m. London time.

08｜The Message Before the Flight｜登机前的交接

On Friday morning, Sarah pulls up a chair.

“Can you explain yesterday's issue without all the implementation details?”

Leo turns his laptop away. “The new version rejected some valid sessions, so affected users were logged out. Rolling back restored the previous behavior.”

“Did we lose any data?”

“We haven't found evidence of data loss in the checks completed so far. The remaining checks are listed in the incident notes.”

“And the next step?”

“We've reproduced the failure. Daniel will review the fix, and I'll document the edge case before I leave.”

At the airport, Leo reads his handover one last time. It has links to the ticket and logs, the current status, and a name beside each next step.

Daniel replies: “Thanks. Safe travels!”

Leo types, “See you online next week.”

His boarding group is called. He closes the laptop before thinking of another sentence to add.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
valid sessions 有效的会话；valid 不等于已经经过验证。
evidence of data loss 数据丢失的证据；evidence 通常不可数。
handover / next steps 交接／后续行动。
an edge case 边界情况；不常出现但需要考虑的输入或条件。

使用提醒：没查出证据与证实不存在是两回事。对非技术同事，先说明用户影响和当前状态。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：用三句向产品经理说明一次故障。写：为未完成事项指定负责人。

参考表达：The release caused some users to be logged out. We rolled back, and the service is stable. Daniel will review the fix; I’ll finish the incident notes today.

09｜You’re Breaking Up｜断断续续的视频会议

Back home, Leo joins a call with the London team. Daniel's picture freezes halfway through a sentence.

“Sorry, you're breaking up. Could you repeat the deadline?”

Daniel speaks again, but Leo catches only “Thursday.”

“Could you put the date and time in the chat?”

A message appears: “Thursday, 15 October, 09:00 London time.”

Leo checks the calendar invitation. “That works for me.”

Sarah starts speaking. Her microphone icon is crossed out.

“I think you're on mute,” Leo says.

She laughs and switches it on. They discuss the remaining work, but one detail needs a longer conversation.

“Let's follow up separately,” Daniel says. “I'll send the context first.”

Before leaving, Leo reads the action items aloud. This time, he confirms who is doing each task as well as when it is due.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
You’re breaking up. 你的声音断断续续；常指连接问题。
You’re on mute. 你的麦克风处于静音状态。
follow up separately 会后另行跟进。
action items / due date 待办事项／应完成的日期。

时间：跨时区约会写明日期和地点时区，通过日历换算；不要把伦敦与国内的时差全年固定为一个值。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：模拟网络不稳，请对方重复日期并发到聊天框。写：补一句会后确认。

参考表达：You’re breaking up. Could you put the deadline in the chat? / To confirm, I’ll send the report before our next call.

10｜Friday Off｜请假之前

The data endpoint is still unavailable on Tuesday. Leo reads the last update, then messages the owner.

“Hi Priya, do you have an updated estimate for the export endpoint? We need it for integration. If it slips past Wednesday, Monday's release may be at risk.”

Priya replies that a sample response is ready, but the endpoint needs another day.

“Thanks. The sample will help us continue. Please let me know if the timeline changes.”

Then Leo opens a different message to his manager.

“I'd like to take Friday off. I'll finish the integration notes on Thursday, and Daniel has agreed to cover urgent questions.”

“Fine with me. Please update the team calendar.”

Leo adds the leave and schedules a short handover. Closing the laptop on Thursday feels easier when nobody has to guess where the work stands.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
an updated estimate 更新后的预估。
slip past Wednesday 推迟到周三之后。
take Friday off 周五请一天假。
cover urgent questions 代为处理紧急问题。

催进度：给出你依赖什么、何时需要、延期会影响什么。避免只发 Any update?，让对方猜上下文。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

写：给依赖团队写一条跟进消息，包含截止时间及延期影响。

参考表达：Could you confirm when the test endpoint will be ready? We need it by Wednesday to finish integration. A later delivery would put Monday’s release at risk.

11｜One Useful Question｜一次反馈谈话

During their monthly conversation, Leo's manager asks what has become easier.

“Small meetings,” Leo says. “But in larger ones, I sometimes wait too long to speak.”

“What makes you wait?”

“I'm trying to make the sentence perfect. By the time it's ready, the topic has changed.”

His manager nods. “Try coming in with one useful question. You don't need a speech.”

“Could you give me feedback on whether my updates are clear?”

“Of course. Your written handovers are already easy to follow.”

A few months later, Leo joins another planning call. He has been offered a year with the London team.

While people discuss the move, he notices something missing from the schedule.

“Can I check one thing before we move on? Who's coordinating the first week's accommodation?”

“Maya,” his manager says. “Good question.”

This time, Leo did not wait.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
give feedback on … 就……给反馈；feedback 通常不可数。
By the time … 等到……的时候。
before we move on 在进入下一个话题之前。
coordinate accommodation 协调住宿安排；accommodation 通常不可数。

插话：Can I check one thing? 是自然的切入方式。先用一句短问句获得发言空间，再补充背景。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：向同事请求一个具体维度的反馈。

参考表达：Could you give me feedback on my meeting updates? I’d like to know whether the main point and next steps are clear.

12｜A Number and an Address｜手机号与地址

On his second arrival in London, Leo buys a SIM card before leaving the airport.

“Prepaid or a monthly plan?” the assistant asks.

“Can I start with prepaid? I'm not sure how much data I'll need.”

She shows him two plans. Leo points to the smaller one.

“Does this renew automatically? And is roaming included?”

They check the terms together. Leo chooses it and saves the confirmation.

Two days later, he asks a bank employee about opening an account.

“What documents would I need?”

“Identification and an accepted proof of address for this account.”

“I'm staying in temporary accommodation. What can I use?”

The employee gives him a list rather than a yes-or-no answer.

Leo takes a photo of it. “Thanks. I'll check what I have. Is there a monthly fee?”

One question leads to another. For once, that feels like progress.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
a prepaid SIM / a monthly plan 预付费 SIM 卡／月度套餐。
data allowance / roaming 套餐流量额度／漫游。
renew automatically 自动续费或续订。
proof of address 地址证明；能接受哪些材料要向具体机构确认。

阅读：在套餐中找费用、流量、续订方式和取消条件。这里练询问方式，不把故事当成开户材料清单。

展开：开口／动笔任务与参考

说：询问套餐是否自动续费，以及如何取消。

参考表达：Does this plan renew automatically? How can I cancel it, and is there a cancellation fee?

13｜The Quiet Flat｜看房时多问一句

The flat looks brighter than the photos. Leo stands near the window and listens. No traffic, just someone closing a door downstairs.

“It's furnished,” the agent says. “Available from Monday.”

“Are utilities included in the rent?”

“Water is. Electricity and internet are separate.”

Leo writes that down. “How much is the deposit?”

The agent shows him the figure. Later, at the kitchen table, they read the draft lease.

“What does this notice period mean for me?” Leo asks.

“It describes how much notice you need to give before ending the tenancy under these terms.”

“And who should I contact if something needs repairing?”

The agent points to the maintenance details.

“Could you email me the draft and a list of all the charges? I'd like to read them before deciding.”

Outside, Leo checks the journey to work. A quiet flat is useful. So is knowing what it will cost.

展开：表达、听辨与使用提醒
英文表达 中文与用法
furnished / unfurnished 带家具的／不带家具的。
utilities / deposit 水电燃气等公用事业服务或费用／押金。
lease / tenancy / notice period 租约／租赁关系或租期／提前通知期限。

2. 容易混淆的意思和句型

英文表达或对比 中文辨析
authentication / authorization 认证回答“你是谁”，授权回答“你能做什么”。
authority / permission authority 可指职权、权威或主管机构；permission 更常用于具体许可、权限。

direct / nonstop direct 不在所有交通语境中都表示“不经停”；想确认航班不经停用 nonstop。

resolve an issue / fix a bug 解决问题／修复缺陷。解决用户问题可能采用临时措施，未必已修好缺陷。

suggest doing / suggest that we do 建议做……／建议我们做……；避免 suggest me to do。

六、听辨、口语与阅读练习

1. 听辨：数字、时间与条件

英文例子 中文与训练重点
thirteen / thirty; fifteen / fifty; eighteen / eighty 十三／三十；十五／五十；十八／八十。结合重音与上下文判断，关键数字务必复述确认。
B / D / P / T; M / N 拼姓名和编号时容易混淆；可说 B as in Bravo（Bravo 的首字母 B）。
sixteen forty / four forty p.m. 16:40／下午 4:40；这是同一时刻的两种读法。
three point five / three hundred and fifty 3.5／350；注意小数点和数量级。
thirteen pounds fifty 13.50 英镑；在价格语境下常省略 pence（便士）。
can / can’t; do / don’t; has / hasn’t 能／不能；做／不做；已经／尚未等对比。没听清否定时，直接确认意思。
only / already / yet / still 仅仅／已经／还、尚未／仍然；会改变范围和状态。
before / after / by / until 在……之前／之后／不晚于／一直到。
if / unless / as long as 如果／除非／只要；听清成立条件。
5 June / June 5 6 月 5 日；跨地区书写尽量写月份名称，避免 05/06 的歧义。

意群练习：Could you check / whether this ticket is valid / for the next train?（可以确认这张票能否坐下一班车吗？）斜杠表示按意义分组，不要求每处明显停顿。重读 ticket、valid、next train 等承载信息的部分。

展开：数字与条件信息听辨练习

请朗读工具或练习伙伴读下面三句；自己暂时不看，记录“原安排、变化、条件”。若自己已经看过，可把数字替换后再练。

  1. Your train was due at ten thirty. It’s now expected at ten forty-five, from platform four.
  2. We can release on Monday if the endpoint is ready by Wednesday.
  3. The room costs one hundred and thirty pounds per night. Breakfast isn’t included.

核对：① 原定 10:30，预计 10:45，4 号站台。② 周一发布的前提是接口最迟周三就绪。③ 每晚 130 英镑，不含早餐。

口头确认示例：So breakfast costs extra, right?（所以早餐需要另付费，对吗？）

英文应变表达 中文与适用场景
